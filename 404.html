What is Capoeira?

Capoeira (pronounced ka-poo-ay-da) is a martial art developed in Brazil by enslaved Africans. It is a communal game in which two opponents play each other inside a circle, formed by the other players who create rhythm for the game by clapping, singing and playing traditional instruments.

  • Afro-Brazilian martial art with over 500 years of history
  • Combines self-defense, acrobatics, dance, and music
  • Full-body workout for all fitness levels
  • Learn traditional instruments and Portuguese songs
  • Join a global community of practitioners

Why ABADÁ-Capoeira?

Part of a Global Family

ABADÁ-Capoeira was founded in 1988 by Mestre Camisa in Rio de Janeiro. The name stands for Associação Brasileira de Apoio e Desenvolvimento da Arte-Capoeira (The Brazilian Association for the Support and Development of the Art of Capoeira).
