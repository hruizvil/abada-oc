User-agent: *
Allow: /

# /waiver is deliberately NOT disallowed here.
# It carries <meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow"> instead.
# Disallowing it would stop crawlers fetching the page at all, which means
# they would never see that noindex tag — and Google can still list a blocked
# URL it has never read. Letting it crawl and read the noindex is what actually
# keeps it out of results.
